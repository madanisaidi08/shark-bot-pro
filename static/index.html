Paste this code directly into the Pine Editor at the bottom of TradingView. It automatically configures buy/sell conditions and sends alerts to your Shark Bot Webhook! 

// © Shark Bot Pro - Automated Binance Spot Strategy Template
//@version=5
indicator("Shark Bot Pro - Strategy Indicator", overlay=true)

// 1. Indicator Parameters (e.g. Fast & Slow EMAs)
fastLength = input.int(9, title="Fast EMA Length")
slowLength = input.int(21, title="Slow EMA Length")
tpPercent  = input.float(2.5, title="Take Profit %")
slPercent  = input.float(1.2, title="Stop Loss %")
capitalPct = input.int(100, title="Capital % Allocation")
secretKey  = input.string("secure_trading_pass_2026", title="Webhook Secret")

// 2. Calculation
fastEMA = ta.ema(close, fastLength)
slowEMA = ta.ema(close, slowLength)

plot(fastEMA, color=color.cyan, title="Fast EMA")
plot(slowEMA, color=color.orange, title="Slow EMA")

// 3. Buy and Sell Conditions
buyCondition  = ta.crossover(fastEMA, slowEMA)
sellCondition = ta.crossunder(fastEMA, slowEMA)

plotshape(buyCondition, title="Buy Signal", style=shape.triangleup, location=location.belowbar, color=color.green, size=size.small, text="BUY")
plotshape(sellCondition, title="Sell Signal", style=shape.triangledown, location=location.abovebar, color=color.red, size=size.small, text="SELL")

// 4. Send Webhook Alerts to Shark Bot Pro
if (buyCondition)
    alert('{"secret": "' + secretKey + '", "action": "BUY", "symbol": "' + syminfo.ticker + '", "tp_percent": ' + str.tostring(tpPercent) + ', "sl_percent": ' + str.tostring(slPercent) + ', "capital_percent": ' + str.tostring(capitalPct) + '}', alert.freq_once_per_bar_close)

if (sellCondition)
    alert('{"secret": "' + secretKey + '", "action": "SELL", "symbol": "' + syminfo.ticker + '"}', alert.freq_once_per_bar_close)

How This Engine Protects & Trades

Dual Action On Rise (أمران متزامنان عند الصعود)

When price hits Level K, it sells Level K-1 for profit (+1%), and simultaneously buys Level K with target Level K+1.

Sequential Single-Buy On Retrace (شراء متسلسل بالهبوط)

When price drops from 10 to 9, it buys Level 9 (because it was freed previously). If it falls to 8, it buys Level 8.

Strict One-Shot Rule (قاعدة تجميد المستوى ومنع التكرار)

If price oscillates around Level 1, it will NEVER buy twice from Level 1 until that trade is sold at profit or stopped out.

100% Guaranteed Capital Protection (أمان رأس المال)

With 10 levels at $20 each, total exposure can never exceed $200 under any market crash or fluctuation.

Trailing Infinity Open Range (نطاق مفتوح لا نهائي)

No fixed ceiling! If price trends upward continuously, the grid generates new zones automatically and rides the trend with pre-placed limit sells.

Pre-placed Limit Sell & Partial Fill Safety (تعليق أمر البيع وإلغاء المتبقي)

Limit sell is instantly pre-placed at the next zone. If an order fills partially and price rallies, remaining buy order is cancelled and actual filled amount is sold at target.

Quick Beginner Manual ("السهل الممتنع")

1. How does Shark Bot Pro work?

Shark Bot connects your TradingView strategy directly to Binance Spot. When your indicator fires a BUY signal, Shark Bot uses your chosen capital percentage (e.g. 100%) to buy immediately, and instantly places Take Profit (TP) and Stop Loss (SL) OCO orders on Binance.

2. What is an OCO Order?

OCO stands for "One-Cancels-the-Other". It places both your Target Profit order and your Stop Loss order at the same time. If the price hits your target, profit is taken and the stop loss is automatically removed. Your funds are protected 24/7 without needing you to watch the screen.

3. How does balance synchronization work?

Whenever you enter or exit a trade, Shark Bot immediately queries Binance and broadcasts your fresh USDT and asset balance via WebSocket. You can also click the "Refresh Now" button at any time to instantly update.
